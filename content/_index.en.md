# Lead Platform Engineer · Data Platform Architect · Distributed Systems

I design and build engineering and data platforms that enable teams to develop, deploy, and scale applications, analytical services, and data-driven products safely and efficiently.

I help transform complex infrastructure into reliable, manageable, and reproducible platform products.

## About Me

I am a Lead Platform Engineer and Data Platform Architect with more than 20 years of experience in IT.

I work at the intersection of platform engineering, distributed systems, data infrastructure, security, and production operations.

I specialize in platforms that have moved beyond the experimental stage: multiple teams begin relying on them, the cost of change increases, and reliability, security, access control, and operational reproducibility become critical.

I combine architectural responsibility with hands-on engineering. I design solutions, build prototypes, integrate components, automate operational processes, and participate directly in bringing systems into production.

## Areas of Expertise

### Platform Engineering

I build internal engineering platforms and self-service capabilities that reduce teams' dependence on manual infrastructure operations.

### Data Platform Architecture

I design platforms for collecting, storing, processing, and serving data for analytics, applications, and AI workloads.

### Security and Access Control

I design identity and access-management models, including RBAC, Policy as Code, and fine-grained access control for data and infrastructure.

### Reliability and Operations

I design observability, operational processes, and architectures that remain reliable under failures, increasing workloads, and continuous change.

### Infrastructure Automation

I automate infrastructure provisioning, configuration, delivery, and operations using Infrastructure as Code and GitOps practices.

## Typical Challenges

I am particularly effective when:

- a prototype or internal project needs to become a production platform;
- multiple teams begin using the same platform;
- manual operations limit development speed;
- changes become expensive and risky;
- architecture formally exists, but decisions are still made situationally;
- there is no consistent model of access or ownership;
- reliability, auditing, and security requirements are increasing;
- data infrastructure needs to support analytics and AI workloads.

In these situations, the core problem is rarely the choice of a specific technology. More often, it is the loss of manageability, architectural context, and clear system ownership.

## Approach

I view architecture not as a collection of diagrams, but as a system of deliberate technical and organizational decisions.

I focus on:

- clear component and ownership boundaries;
- reproducible deployment and operations;
- automation of repetitive processes;
- system observability;
- safe and controlled changes;
- reducing dependency on individual specialists;
- enabling teams to evolve the platform without constant manual intervention.

My goal is not simply to introduce another technology. It is to build a system that teams can operate, understand, and evolve over the long term.
